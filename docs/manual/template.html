AJ Box manual

A loop toy that lives on an M-VAVE FM-1. One hand picks a drum beat, the other picks a tune, and whatever she presses stays in time. Tap any key, button or knob on the picture to see its job.

Where this stands (15 Nov 2026, night). AJ Box is on the FM-1. This describes the seventh build (Build ID b7fae6f4…), which is not recorded as installed yet. Over the fourth build, the last one recorded as installed, it adds the speed pop-up, the battery picture, saving in silence and a fix for a note that could stick. The screen pictures come from the desktop simulator running the same code.

What the screen shows

Pictures, not words. Pink is drums, purple is melody. These are the real 240 × 240 screens.

The loops

96 loops: 6 pages, each with 8 drum loops and 8 melodies. The numbers are the white keys, counted from the left on each side. The Back and Next keys turn the page for one side only, so any drum page can go with any melody page. Around the World and Animal Choir, with the four Zelda tunes, are page 1.

The 8 one-shots

Most drums are made by the FM-1's drum machine. These 8 are short sounds of their own, made by synthesis (nothing was recorded), and five drum loops are built from them. Press play to hear each one. They sit in the FM-1's sample bank, apart from the firmware, so installing a new build leaves them alone.

Swapping sounds

PRESETS: 12 drum kits

Same beat, new drums. Every page starts on its own kit; turning PRESETS steps through all of them. The one-shots keep their own sound.

    ALGORITHM: 20 instruments

    Same tune, new instrument. A new melody loop goes back to its own instrument.

      Moods

      Turn KNOB 4. Each click steps to the next Mood, or the one before, round and round, and shows its picture. The melody moves to a different set of 5 notes on the next bar, so every tune works in every mood. The four Zelda tunes stay as written.

      The lights

      White keys

      The key of the loop that is playing is lit, while its page is the one shown. A Dice pick blinks twice first.

      Black keys

      Fill, Fast and the two Mute keys are lit while held.

      Buttons

      An effect button is lit while its effect is on. PLAY/STOP is lit while music plays, REC while recording, ARP while the jam replays.

      OCT− and OCT+

      One is lit when the melody is below or above its normal octave.

      Good to know

      It always lands in time

      A new loop joins on the step the beat is on. Nothing waits for the next bar unless parent mode says so. A new Mood always starts on the next bar.

      Two at most

      One drum loop and one melody. A new key on the same side swaps; the playing key again stops that side on the next beat.

      Hearts

      SAVE keeps the drum, melody, sounds, speed and effects as a heart. There are 8; a ninth replaces the oldest. SEQ steps through them.

      Her jam

      REC records everything she presses and turns for up to 2 minutes. ARP plays it back. A new jam replaces the old one.

      It remembers

      Pages, the last pair, sounds, speed and effects come back after switching off. They are saved 5 seconds after the music stops, so switching off mid-song brings back what was there the last time it stopped. Nothing plays until she presses a key or PLAY/STOP.

      A tiny gap on SAVE

      Saving cuts the sound for a moment. A heart, and the end of a recording, save straight away, so the gap is heard then. Nothing else saves while music plays.

      The battery

      Three bars at the top left: three from about two thirds full, two from a third, one when nearly empty. With none left the sleepy battery shows once: time to charge.

      Always a way back

      PLAY/STOP is silence. HOME switches every effect off and puts the filter back in the middle.

      Never too loud

      A limiter sits on the output all the time, and each effect has a safe top setting.

      If a save fails

      A heart that could not be saved shows as a dark, empty heart. Parent mode says "Not saved". Nothing is shown as kept unless it was read back.
