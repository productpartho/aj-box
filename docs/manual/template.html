AJ Box manual

A loop toy that lives on an M-VAVE FM-1. One hand picks a drum beat, the other picks a tune, and whatever she presses stays in time. Tap any key, button or knob on the picture to see its job.

Where this stands (15 Nov 2026). This describes AJ Box as it runs in the desktop simulator. It is not on the FM-1 yet, so the last section lists what can still change once it is.

What the screen shows

Pictures, not words. Pink is drums, purple is melody. These are the real 240 × 240 screens.

The loops

96 loops: 6 pages, each with 8 drum loops and 8 melodies. The numbers are the white keys, counted from the left on each side. The Back and Next keys turn the page for one side only, so any drum page can go with any melody page.

The 8 one-shots

Most drums are made by the FM-1's drum machine. These 8 are short sounds of their own, made by synthesis (nothing was recorded), and five drum loops are built from them. Press play to hear each one.

Swapping sounds

PRESETS: 12 drum kits

Same beat, new drums. Every page starts on its own kit; turning PRESETS steps through all of them. The one-shots keep their own sound.

    ALGORITHM: 20 instruments

    Same tune, new instrument. A new melody loop goes back to its own instrument.

      Moods

      Hold OCT− or OCT+ and turn ALGORITHM. The melody moves to a different set of 5 notes on the next bar, so every tune works in every mood. The four Zelda tunes stay as written.

      Good to know

      It always lands in time

      A new loop joins on the step the beat is on. Nothing waits for the next bar unless parent mode says so.

      Two at most

      One drum loop and one melody. A new key on the same side swaps; the playing key again stops that side on the next beat.

      Hearts

      SAVE keeps the drum, melody, sounds, speed and effects as a heart. There are 8; a ninth replaces the oldest. SEQ steps through them.

      Her jam

      REC records everything she presses and turns for up to 2 minutes. ARP plays it back. A new jam replaces the old one.

      It remembers

      Pages, the last pair, sounds, speed and effects come back after switching off. Nothing plays until she presses a key or PLAY/STOP.

      Always a way back

      PLAY/STOP is silence. HOME switches every effect off and puts the filter back in the middle.

      Never too loud

      A limiter sits on the output all the time, and each effect has a safe top setting.

      If a save fails

      A heart that could not be saved shows as a dark, empty heart. Parent mode says "Not saved". Nothing is shown as kept unless it was read back.

      Not settled until it is on the FM-1

      • Moving around parent mode. HOME + SAVE held for 2 seconds opens it. Which controls step through the rows and change them is still to be decided.
      • The battery picture. The readings that mean 1, 2 or 3 bars and "low" have to be measured on the unit.
      • Key lights. The plan is: the playing key stays lit, a Dice pick blinks twice.
      • How fast SELECT moves. The plan is 2 BPM a click, more when turned quickly.
      • Stickers and knob caps. A picture strip over the black keys and coloured caps for KNOB 1 to 4 are planned, not made.
      • Timing and loudness on the real speaker. Checked on the desktop only so far.
